About Atlantic Holdings

One point of contact, from foundations to fittings

Atlantic Holdings (Private) Limited is a Zimbabwean company registered under the Companies and Other Business Entities Act [Chapter 24:31]. We were built around a simple idea: construction projects move faster when the people building them can also source their equipment, materials and hardware from one trusted supplier.

From our office at Hamilton Heights, corner Mazowe and Fife Avenue, Harare, we support contractors, developers and individual clients across the full construction value chain.

What we do

Four services, one supplier

Each line stands on its own, and works even better together when a project needs more than one at a time.

Construction

General building and civil works — site management, project documentation, tendering and contract execution for residential, commercial and institutional projects.

Equipment Hire

Plant and equipment hire for contractors and clients undertaking earthworks and civil works — without the capital cost of owning machinery outright.

Material Supply

Sourcing and supply of bulk construction materials — aggregates, cement, steel and related building materials — delivered on time to site.

Hardware

General hardware and building supplies for contractors, tradespeople and individual clients — from fixings to finishing materials.
